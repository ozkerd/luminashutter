Frequently Asked Questions

How does Light Trails mode work?
Light Trails uses a 32-bit float maximum-luminance blending pipeline. Moving lights such as automotive headlights, taillights, and fireworks streak across the frame while dark backgrounds remain crisp and noise-free.
How does Crowd Eraser remove tourists and pedestrians?
Crowd Eraser calculates a temporal running median filter across incoming frames. Stationary structures (monuments, architecture, landscapes) are mathematically preserved while transient subjects in motion are smoothly erased from the final composite.
Do I need a tripod to take long exposures?
While a tripod guarantees maximum sharpness, LuminaShutter integrates real-time gyro and accelerometer motion stabilization to counteract handheld tremors for exposures up to 5–9 seconds.
How do I restore my Lifetime purchase?
Open the settings dial or tap the info icon at the top of the camera viewfinder to open the LuminaShutter Lifetime card, then tap "Restore Purchases". Apple StoreKit will instantly verify your Apple ID entitlement.
